Multimodal Emotion Intelligence — Guide

Human emotion is expressed across two distinct channels simultaneously: visual facial kinematics and acoustic vocal prosody.

1. Facial Analysis (70% Weight): 468 landmarks track zygomatic smile contours, eye aspect ratio (EAR), and brow furrowing in real-time.
2. Voice Modulation (30% Weight): Acoustic prosody analysis evaluates vocal pitch, cadence, and frequency energy via microphone.
3. Decision Fusion Formula: $P_{\text{fused}} = 0.70 \cdot P_{\text{face}} + 0.30 \cdot P_{\text{voice}}$. Dissonance detects sarcasm or masked affect.
