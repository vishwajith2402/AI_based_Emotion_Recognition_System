📘 Beginner's Guide: How This AI Works

Humans express feelings through two channels simultaneously: how our face looks, and how our voice sounds. Sometimes they agree, and sometimes they contradict each other!

1. Facial CNN (Eyes & Mouth): Scans 48×48 face crops looking for smiles, raised eyebrows, or frowns.
2. Speech BiLSTM (Voice Tone): Analyzes sound wave pitch and cadence (MFCC) forward and backward in time.
3. Decision Fusion Formula: $P_{\text{final}} = w_{\text{face}} \cdot P_{\text{face}} + w_{\text{speech}} \cdot P_{\text{speech}}$. If both disagree, it alerts you of a Modality Conflict!
